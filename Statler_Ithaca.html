The Hotel

A press house on the gorge, rebuilt for the people who visit Ithaca

The Cascadilla Press printed lecture notes, alumni journals and seed catalogues for the university and the farms of the Finger Lakes from 1911 until 1978. The brick hall sat empty on the gorge rim for four decades before a five-year restoration reopened it as The Statler, Ithaca in May 2024.

The pressroom is now the lobby and Library Bar, with the original cast-iron columns and a working 1923 Linotype by the fireplace. A 1920s residence wing on the east side holds the larger suites. Every room on the north side looks down into the gorge, and the top floors see across to Cayuga Lake.

The Statler, Ithaca is the newest sister to The Statler, London, Paris and New York and Statler ME, Riyadh. It is built for campus visits, faculty stays, Commencement weekends, wine-country trips and small conferences that want a walkable university town.

  • I.Gorge-rim location: the Cascadilla Gorge trail starts at the hotel gate and climbs to Collegetown.
  • II.Complimentary shuttle to Ho Plaza on Central Campus every 20 minutes, 7:00 AM to 11:00 PM.
  • III.Finger Lakes kitchen and cellar: more than 80 local Rieslings, ciders and cheeses on the menus.

Is breakfast included?

Breakfast is included in package rates and for Reading Room guests. Otherwise the Linotype breakfast is $28 per adult and $14 for children under 12, and Galley Bakery opens at 6:00 AM for grab-and-go.

How do I get to Cornell's campus?

It is a 10-minute walk up the Cascadilla Gorge trail or College Avenue to the Arts Quad. Our complimentary shuttle runs to Ho Plaza every 20 minutes from 7:00 AM to 11:00 PM.

Do you offer airport transfers?

Yes. Ithaca Tompkins (ITH) is 15 minutes away and transfers are $45 each way by sedan. Syracuse (SYR) is about 75 minutes, $225 each way. Book through the concierge at least 24 hours ahead.

Do I earn loyalty points?

Statler Circle members earn 10 points per dollar on room rate and dining across all Statler hotels, including London, Paris, New York and Riyadh. Gold members get Reading Room access and a guaranteed 2:00 PM check-out.

What accessibility features do you have?

Six accessible rooms, three with roll-in showers, step-free access throughout including the terrace, a pool lift, and hearing-accessibility kits on request. Call +1 (607) 555-0142 to confirm the right room for your needs.
