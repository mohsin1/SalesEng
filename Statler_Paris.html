The Hotel

A Belle Époque bank headquarters, reopened as the Statler flagship

The Statler, Paris occupies the former headquarters of Banque Vendôme, a Belle Époque banking house built in 1901 on the Rue de la Paix in the 2nd arrondissement, moments from Place Vendôme and the Opéra Garnier. Reopened in 2024 as the third flagship of Statler Hotels — sister property to Statler ME in Riyadh and The Statler, London — the building's stone facade, gilded banking hall and wrought-iron stair have been restored and paired with a contemporary glass-atrium wing to the rear, together forming 350 rooms and suites, six restaurants and bars, and a rooftop pool.

The hotel is a short walk from the Opéra and Madeleine métro stations, close to the Jardin des Tuileries, the Louvre and the grands magasins of Boulevard Haussmann.

  • II.The former currency vault, one level below ground, has been reworked into the Statler Spa
  • III.A glass-roofed rooftop extension houses Piscine Vendôme and its poolside bistro, La Piscine, with views over the Paris rooftops

Is breakfast included?

Not by default. Continental or full breakfast from €36/guest at Brasserie Chaillot; included in select packages.

Is room service available?

Yes — 24 hours, from all six restaurant and bar menus.

Does the hotel have a loyalty programme?

Yes — Statler Privé (Silver, Gold, Platinum, Statler Elite), the same chain-wide programme as Statler ME in Riyadh and The Statler, London, free to join.

Is there parking?

Limited valet parking, €70/night; central Paris street parking is very restricted, so most guests arrive by rail, métro or taxi.
