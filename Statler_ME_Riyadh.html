The Hotel

Contemporary Najdi design, forty-two floors above the city

Statler ME, Riyadh is the Middle East flagship of Statler Hotels, occupying the upper floors of Statler Tower in Riyadh's Al Olaya business district, minutes from the King Abdullah Financial District. The design draws on the geometric patterns of traditional Najdi sadu weaving and the mudbrick architecture of historic Diriyah, reinterpreted through a contemporary glass and stone tower — opened in 2023 as part of the Kingdom's Vision 2030 expansion of Riyadh's hospitality sector.

All six restaurants, the rooftop lounge and in-room dining are halal-certified; no pork or alcohol is served anywhere on the property, in line with Saudi Arabian law. Prayer facilities and Qibla orientation are provided in every room, and a dedicated prayer floor is available to all guests.
