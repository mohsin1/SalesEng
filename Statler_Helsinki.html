The Hotel

A 1912 granite bank, reopened as the Statler's Nordic home

The Statler, Helsinki occupies the former Pohjola Merchant Bank, a granite-and-copper Jugendstil building completed in 1912 on Pohjoisesplanadi, facing the lime trees of Esplanadi Park. Reopened in 2025 as the Nordic home of Statler Hotels — sister to the Statler hotels across Europe, New York and Riyadh — its carved granite portal, stained-glass banking hall and bronze-gated vaults have been restored around 190 rooms and suites.

The hotel is two minutes on foot from Market Square and the South Harbour ferries to Suomenlinna, five minutes from Senate Square and Helsinki Cathedral, and ten minutes from the Design District, the Oodi library and Helsinki Central Station, with the airport train every ten minutes.

  • I.The stained-glass banking hall, with its original 1912 granite columns, is now the lobby and Esplanadi Grill
  • II.The bank's granite vaults hold Löyly Spa — three saunas, an ice-cold plunge and the 18-metre Vault Pool
  • III.Lumo, on the eighth-floor copper roof, looks over the South Harbour to Suomenlinna

Is breakfast included?

Not by default. The Nordic breakfast buffet at Esplanadi Grill is €32; included with Statler Club rooms, suites and most packages.

How do I get from the airport?

The I/P commuter train runs from Helsinki-Vantaa to Central Station every 10 minutes (about 30 min, €4.10), then a 10-minute walk or tram. A house car can be arranged from €95.

Does the hotel have a loyalty programme?

Yes — Statler Privé (Silver, Gold, Platinum, Statler Elite), shared across all Statler hotels worldwide.

Is the hotel accessible?

Yes — step-free entrance on Mikonkatu, lifts to all floors and the spa, and 10 accessible rooms with roll-in showers. A hoist is available at the Vault Pool.

How do I get to Suomenlinna?

The public ferry leaves Market Square every 20–40 minutes, a two-minute walk from the hotel (15 min crossing, HSL ticket). The concierge can also arrange a private boat.
