The Resort

A private island on the Great Barrier Reef

The Aurelius Regent occupies the whole of Aurelius Cay, a private island in the Coral Sea fringed by its own reef, reached by a twenty-minute launch transfer from the Aurelius Regent Mission Beach Mainland Lounge. Rooms, suites and beach villas sit low among the palms and rainforest canopy, built on the principle that nothing on the island should stand taller than the trees around it.

The island's fringing reef is part of the Great Barrier Reef Marine Park. Snorkelling, diving and reef charters leave directly from the resort's own jetty, and walking trails maintained with the local national parks service run the length of the island's rainforest interior.

Is breakfast included?

Not by default. À la carte or a breakfast package from AUD 55/guest; included in select packages.

What if the launch can't run due to weather?

The resort operates its own launch and helicopter transfer. If conditions prevent the boat crossing, guests are moved to the next available crossing or a helicopter transfer at no charge.

Does the resort have a loyalty programme?

Yes — Aurelius Privé (Silver, Gold, Platinum, Aurelius Elite), free to join.

Is the resort wheelchair accessible?

Selected villas and public areas are accessible; please advise the resort in advance of any specific requirements.
