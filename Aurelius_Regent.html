The Resort

A private island on the Great Barrier Reef

The Aurelius Regent occupies the whole of Aurelius Cay, a 42-hectare private island in the Coral Sea fringed by its own reef, reached by a twenty-minute launch from the resort's Mission Beach Mainland Lounge. Rooms, suites and beach villas sit low among the palms and rainforest canopy, built on the principle that nothing on the island should stand taller than the trees around it.

The island's fringing reef is part of the Great Barrier Reef Marine Park. Snorkelling, diving and outer-reef charters leave directly from the resort jetty, and walking trails maintained with Queensland Parks run the length of the rainforest interior. The resort is carbon-neutral, runs on solar power and desalinates its own water.

Is breakfast included?

Yes — breakfast at Sunrise Terrace (or in-villa for Beachfront Villas and above) is included in all rates. Full-board and all-inclusive plans are available from AUD 280 per adult per day.

What if the launch can't run due to weather?

The resort operates its own launch and helicopter. If conditions prevent the crossing, guests are moved to the next available crossing or a helicopter transfer at no charge, and the Mainland Lounge stays open until the last guest crosses.

When is the best time to visit?

May to October is dry and mild (24–28°C). November to April is the warm wet season; marine stinger season runs roughly November–May, and complimentary stinger suits are provided for swimming and snorkelling.

Does the resort have a loyalty programme?

Yes — Aurelius Privé (Silver, Gold, Platinum, Aurelius Elite), free to join.

Is the resort wheelchair accessible?

Four rooms and the main public areas are accessible, and the launch has a level-boarding ramp. Please advise the resort of any requirements in advance.
