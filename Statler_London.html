The Hotel

A Grade II-listed exchange, reopened as the Statler flagship

The Statler, London occupies the former Cornhill Marine & General Exchange, a Grade II-listed 1920s insurance hall in the City of London. Reopened in 2021 as the European flagship of Statler Hotels — sister property to Statler ME in Riyadh — the building's Portland stone facade, marble banking hall and wrought-iron stair have been restored and reworked around 190 rooms and suites, five restaurants, and a ballroom beneath the original trading-floor skylight.

The hotel is a short walk from Bank and Liverpool Street stations, in the heart of the Square Mile, close to Leadenhall Market and the Guildhall.

Is breakfast included?

Not by default. Full English or continental from £32/guest at The Exchange Café; included in select packages.

Is room service available?

Yes — 24 hours, from all five restaurant menus.

Does the hotel have a loyalty programme?

Yes — Statler Privé (Silver, Gold, Platinum, Statler Elite), the same chain-wide programme as Statler ME in Riyadh, free to join.

Is there parking?

Limited valet parking, £65/night; City of London street parking is very restricted, so most guests arrive by rail, Tube or taxi.
