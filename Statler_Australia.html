The Hotel

A heritage customs house, eighteen storeys above the harbour

Statler Australia occupies the restored Customs House on Circular Quay, its 1890s Pyrmont-sandstone façade preserved beneath a contemporary glass tower added in 2021. The design pairs the building's original ironbark beams and stone arches with brass, eucalyptus timber and quartz — a deliberate conversation between the colonial-era wharf and the harbour skyline it now overlooks.

The lobby hosts a rotating exhibition of contemporary Australian art, developed in partnership with Sydney galleries and First Nations–owned art centres; works are available through the hotel's concierge.
