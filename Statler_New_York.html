The Hotel

A 1929 railroad landmark, reopened as the Statler's American flagship

The Statler, New York occupies the former Hudson & Lexington Railroad Company headquarters, a 28-storey Art Deco tower completed in 1929 on Vanderbilt Place, directly across from Grand Central Terminal. Reopened in 2024 as the North American flagship of Statler Hotels — sister property to The Statler, London, The Statler, Paris and Statler ME in Riyadh — its limestone setbacks, bronze elevator doors and terrazzo concourse have been restored around 420 rooms and suites.

The hotel sits in the heart of Midtown, a two-minute walk from Grand Central, five minutes from Bryant Park and the New York Public Library, and ten minutes from Fifth Avenue and Rockefeller Center.

  • I.The original 1929 ticketing hall, with its bronze clock and coffered ceiling, is now the hotel lobby and reception
  • II.The former railroad directors' boardroom on the 27th floor is the Vanderbilt Club Lounge
  • III.The Grand Central Ballroom is the largest pillarless hotel ballroom in East Midtown

Is breakfast included?

Not by default. Breakfast at The Concourse is $38 buffet or à la carte; included with Vanderbilt Club rooms, suites and select packages.

How do I get from the airports?

JFK ~45–60 min and LaGuardia ~25–35 min by car; Newark ~45 min. The hotel can arrange a house car (from $165 JFK / $125 LGA). Metro-North and the subway stop at Grand Central across the street.

Does the hotel have a loyalty programme?

Yes — Statler Privé (Silver, Gold, Platinum, Statler Elite), shared across all Statler hotels including London, Paris and Riyadh. Free to join.

Is the hotel accessible?

Yes — 21 ADA-accessible rooms (including roll-in showers), step-free entrance on Vanderbilt Place, and pool hoist access. Please advise any requirements in advance.

Can I store luggage?

Yes — complimentary luggage storage at the bell desk before check-in and after check-out, 24 hours.
